# Plan

## Steps

0. Read `/core:reference` unless already read.
1. What. The target state in observable terms: what exists when the picture
   is real, and what is explicitly out.
2. Why. One commitment per line, one claim per commitment. What it buys
   goes on its own line. An inference drawn from a commitment is a
   commitment of its own.
3. Open questions. A commitment the user did not take up is open, whatever else
   they approved. Silence is not approval.
4. Record. Plan lives in the repo, free-form. `/core:commit` as `[DOCS]` on `develop`.

## Rules

- The Plan is a target: not a route, not a status.
- Revised when the picture or one of its reasons changes.
- Investigate only enough to know the picture is buildable. Scratchpad spikes only.
